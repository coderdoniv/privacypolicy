Privacy policy

Abracadigit: Number Tricks · Last updated 26 October 2026

Abracadigit is a number-tricks app for children aged about 8 to 11, made to be handed to a child with confidence. This page explains what the app keeps, what it shares, and what you can do about it. It is written for parents and carers.

The short version

  • The app collects no personal information.
  • Nothing leaves the device. The app has no network connection and sends no data anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases.
  • The app contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.
  • The child only ever types numbers.

Who we are

Abracadigit is published by vbverse. You can contact us at support@vbverse.com.

What the app keeps on your device

To remember the child's progress, the app stores these things in a small database file in its private storage on the device:

  • the stars earned for each challenge, and the dates on which each challenge was completed (day, month and year only);
  • whether today's Trick of the Day has been done;
  • whether the Welcome screen has been seen.

None of this includes a name, an age, an account, a device identifier, a location or any free text. It cannot be used to find or identify the child. It is kept only on the device.

What the app does not collect or share

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It does not:

  • connect to the internet or any server, or send any data from the device;
  • ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or any other device permission;
  • show advertising, or use an advertising identifier;
  • track the child or the device across apps or websites;
  • record how the child uses the app for analytics or crash reporting.

Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is no profile of the child for anyone to build.

Links to other websites

The Parent Area contains links to this privacy policy, the terms of use, the support page and an email address. These open in your web browser or mail app, outside Abracadigit, and are protected by a grown-up check (a sum to answer). Once you leave the app, the websites and mail services you use have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children's privacy

Abracadigit is designed for children, and it does not ask a child for any personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Because the app collects none, there is no account to create and no information to ask a parent to approve. Parents can see what the app keeps in the Parent Area, reset it at any time, or delete the app to remove everything it saved.

How long information is kept

  • Progress (stars, completion dates and the Trick of the Day record) stays on the device until a parent resets progress in the Parent Area, or until the app is uninstalled.
  • The Welcome flag stays until the app is uninstalled.
  • On Android, progress is excluded from cloud backups. On Apple devices, the operating system's device backup may include the app's stored data. That data stays on your own device and your own backup, and is not personal information in the sense used here.

Your choices and rights

  • See what is kept: the Parent Area shows the stars, the creatures discovered and the days played.
  • Erase progress: Reset progress in the Parent Area removes every star, every discovered creature and every day played.
  • Remove everything: uninstalling the app deletes all the data it saved on the device.
  • Ask us: you can write to support@vbverse.com with any question about this policy. Because we hold no personal information about you or your child, we cannot look anything up for you, but we will answer your question.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over personal information. Because Abracadigit holds none, those rights have nothing to act on here.

Security

The app keeps its data only on the device, in private app storage that other apps cannot read. It does not contain any passwords or access keys.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to collect or share information, this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date at the top will change. Any such change will be described in the app's store listing.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of New Zealand.
