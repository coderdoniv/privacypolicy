Frequently asked questions

Do I need the internet to use Abracadigit?

No. Every story, challenge and card works offline. The app does not connect to the internet.

Does the app show adverts or ask me to buy anything?

No. There are no adverts, no in-app purchases and no accounts to create.

Does the app collect information about my child?

No. Progress (stars, the creatures discovered and the days played) is kept only on the device. Read the privacy policy for the full details.

How do I open the Parent Area?

On the home screen, tap For grown-ups. You will be asked a multiplication question written in words, such as "What is forty-four times …?". Type the answer on the number keys and tap Check. If the answer is not right, you get a different question, and you can try as many times as you like.

How do I reset my child's progress?

Open the Parent Area and tap Reset progress. A box asks you to confirm. Cancel is selected first, so a stray tap does not erase anything. Resetting removes every star, every discovered creature and every day played on this device, and cannot be undone.

How do I delete everything the app has saved?

Uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all the data it saved on the device.

My child's number did not work in a trick. What now?

Some numbers do not suit a particular trick. For example, the 1089 trick needs a 3-digit number whose first and last digits differ by at least 2. The app explains why and suggests a number that works. There is no fail screen and no timer, so your child can simply try again.

My child is stuck on a challenge.

Every challenge has hints. In the number-machine challenges, your child can tap Show me to see a step. A wrong pick or answer gives a hint and another try. Nothing is timed, and there are no lives.

Does progress run out, or can my child lose a streak?

No. The Magic Shelf counts the days played and never shows a broken streak. A day off loses nothing.

Can I use one shared progress for several children?

Yes. Progress is shared across the device, so every child who uses it sees the same stars and cards.

Where do I find the version number?

In the Parent Area, under About Abracadigit.

Still need help?

Write to support@vbverse.com. Please include the device you use and the version number, and describe what happened.
