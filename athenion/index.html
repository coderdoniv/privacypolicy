Privacy Policy for Athenion

Effective Date: 29th March 2026

Last Updated: 29th March 2026

Athenion is a productivity and time-tracking application designed to help users manage tasks, projects, and generate insights using optional AI integrations.

Athenion follows a privacy-first approach. Your data remains under your control and is primarily stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Athenion is designed to minimize data collection.

  • User Data: Tasks, projects, time logs, and notes created by the user.
  • Integration Data: Data imported from third-party services such as Jira or Todoist (only with user authorization).
  • AI Data: Data explicitly sent by the user for AI-generated reports.

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • App usage data (features used, screens visited)
  • Crash reports and diagnostics

This data is collected in an aggregated and non-identifiable manner where possible.

2. Firebase Services

Athenion uses Firebase services provided by Google to support core functionality.

2.1 Firebase Analytics

  • Used to understand how users interact with the app
  • Helps improve features and user experience
  • Data is aggregated and not used to personally identify users

2.2 Firebase Authentication

  • Used for optional user login
  • May include Email, Google, Apple, or other providers
  • Authentication is securely handled by Firebase

2.3 Crash Reporting

  • Used to identify and fix application crashes
  • No sensitive personal data is intentionally collected

3. Data Storage

  • All primary user data is stored locally on your device.
  • Athenion does not store your personal data on external servers by default.

Cloud Backup (Optional)

  • Athenion may provide an optional cloud backup feature
  • This feature is user-controlled and optional
  • Backup data may include tasks, projects, and app-related data
  • Data is securely transmitted and stored via third-party services

You are responsible for managing and deleting your backups if required.

4. AI Features (Bring Your Own Token - BYOT)

Athenion allows users to connect their own AI service providers.

  • You must provide your own API keys (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, etc.)
  • API keys are stored locally on your device
  • Athenion does not store or transmit your API keys to our servers

When using AI features:

  • Selected data is sent directly to your configured AI provider
  • Processing is governed by the provider’s privacy policy

5. Third-Party Integrations

Athenion may integrate with:

  • Jira
  • Todoist
  • Other supported platforms (future)
  • Access is limited to required data only
  • Authentication is handled securely via the provider
  • Credentials are not stored on our servers

6. Data Sharing

Athenion does not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

Data is only shared:

  • When explicitly triggered by the user
  • With services configured by the user (AI, integrations, backup)
  • With service providers (e.g., Firebase) to operate the app
  • When required by law

7. Data Retention

  • Local data remains until deleted by the user
  • Cloud backups remain until removed by the user
  • Analytics data is retained according to Firebase policies

8. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to processing

You can:

  • Delete data by uninstalling the app
  • Disable integrations at any time
  • Manage permissions via your device settings

9. Security

  • Local-first data storage ensures user control
  • Secure communication with third-party services
  • Industry-standard security practices are followed

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

Some third-party services (e.g., Firebase) may process data outside your country. Appropriate safeguards are applied where required.

11. Children's Privacy

Athenion is not intended for children under 13 (or applicable age in your region).

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date.
