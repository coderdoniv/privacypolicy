Privacy Policy for Athenion

Effective Date: 29th March 2026

Athenion is a productivity and time-tracking application designed to help users manage tasks, projects, and generate insights using optional AI integrations.

Athenion follows a privacy-first approach. Your data remains under your control and is primarily stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Athenion is designed to minimize data collection.

  • User Data: Tasks, projects, time logs, and notes created by the user.
  • Integration Data: Data imported from third-party services such as Jira or Todoist (only with user authorization).
  • AI Data: Data explicitly sent by the user for AI-generated reports.

We do not collect personal data unless explicitly required for a feature.

2. Data Storage

  • All primary user data is stored locally on your device.
  • Athenion does not store your personal data on external servers by default.
  • You are responsible for managing backups of your data.

3. AI Features (Bring Your Own Token - BYOT)

Athenion allows users to connect their own AI service providers.

  • You must provide your own API keys for AI services (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, or other providers).
  • These API keys are stored locally on your device.
  • Athenion does not store or transmit your API keys to any external servers controlled by us.

When using AI features:

  • Selected data is sent directly to the configured AI provider.
  • Data processing is governed by the privacy policy of the respective AI provider.

4. Third-Party Integrations

Athenion may integrate with third-party services such as:

  • Jira
  • Todoist
  • Other task management platforms (future support)

When connected:

  • Athenion accesses only the data required for functionality.
  • Authentication is handled securely via the provider.
  • We do not store your third-party credentials on external servers.

5. Data Sharing

Athenion does not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

Data is only shared:

  • When explicitly triggered by the user (e.g., AI report generation)
  • With third-party services configured by the user

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • Local storage ensures user control
  • Secure communication with third-party services

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Responsibilities

  • Keep your device secure
  • Protect your API keys
  • Review third-party privacy policies before use

8. Children's Privacy

Athenion is not intended for children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Updates will be reflected with a revised effective date.
