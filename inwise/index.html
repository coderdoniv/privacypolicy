Privacy Policy for Inwise

Effective Date: 9 November 2026
Last Updated: 9 November 2026

Inwise is a phone app for the things a household owns. It keeps a record of each thing, with its receipt, documents, warranty, service history and reminders. Inwise is published by vbverse.

Inwise is built to keep your information on your phone. It has no account, no sign-in of its own, no server of its own, no advertising and no tracking. This policy says what the app keeps, what leaves your phone, and why, including the parts that are sent to services you choose or that are part of the phone and Google's own tools.

1. Information Kept on Your Phone

Everything you enter is stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. This includes:

  • what you own: names, categories, rooms, places, prices, purchase dates, serial numbers, warranties, guarantees, insurance and service contracts, maintenance notes and other notes;
  • photos and files you add: pictures of items, receipts and documents, and the original receipt or document files;
  • the text Inwise reads from a receipt or document, which it uses to fill in the record;
  • reminders you have set;
  • pictures and manuals, videos and reviews that you choose to keep with an item, and any answers to your questions that you ask in the app.

Inwise does not send this information to Inwise, because Inwise has no server to receive it. You can see and change it in the app, delete any of it, and export all of it in one file from Settings, under Your data.

2. Reading Receipts and Documents on Your Phone

Inwise reads text in receipts and documents with Google ML Kit text recognition, and separates the subject of a photo from its background with Google ML Kit subject segmentation. These models run on your phone, and the pictures and their text are processed on your phone. However, the ML Kit software also sends diagnostic information to Google, as described in section 4.

3. Services You Choose to Turn On

Inwise works without any of the services below. Each one is off until you set it up yourself, and each one uses your own account with that provider.

Your own AI provider

If you add your own API key for an AI provider in Settings, Inwise can use that provider to suggest details from a receipt, to read pictures, and to answer questions about your things. The providers you can choose are Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Groq, xAI Grok and Ollama, or a custom address you type in, such as a self-hosted server or another service that offers an OpenAI-compatible interface.

Inwise sends the request to that provider only after you turn on "Let my AI see receipts" (for receipts, documents and pictures) or "Let my AI answer my questions" (for questions about your things). Both switches are off when you first use the app. What is sent is the text and pictures needed for that request, which can include item names, prices, shop names, dates, notes and the receipt or document. Your provider keeps the request under its own terms and its own retention period, not under this policy. Inwise does not receive the reply; it is kept on your phone.

Your own web search

If you add a Serper or Brave Search key, Inwise can look for a picture of an item and for pages about it. Inwise sends only the brand, model and name of the item, and only after you turn on "Search with my things' names". The search provider keeps the request under its own terms.

When you open a page that a search found, your phone connects directly to that website, as any browser does. The website's operator can see your phone's network address and the page you asked for. Inwise does not load pages inside the app.

Google Drive backup (Android only)

If you connect Google Drive in Settings, Inwise saves a backup of your records and their files to a hidden folder that only Inwise can use in your own Google Drive (the app data scope). Backups are made weekly and on request. The backup does not include your AI or search keys. Inwise keeps the Google account email shown in its settings, so you can see which Drive is connected. The backup is stored by Google under your Google account and Google's terms. Inwise does not receive a copy. You can delete the backup in Google Drive, and "Disconnect Drive" in Settings ends the link.

Android's own backup and phone transfer

On Android, the phone's own backup and device-to-device transfer can copy Inwise's records and files to your Google account. Your AI and search keys are kept out of that copy. This is handled by Android and Google, not by Inwise.

Feedback you send us

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own mail app with a message addressed to support@vbverse.com. The message already holds the Inwise version, the build and your phone's system version. You can edit or delete any line before you send it, and Inwise sends nothing itself. If you send us feedback or write to us, we receive your email address, what you write and, if you leave it in, that version line. We use them only to answer you and to improve Inwise, keep them in our support mailbox for as long as needed for that (at most two years), and never share or sell them. To have a message deleted, write to support@vbverse.com.

4. Google ML Kit Diagnostics

Google ML Kit, used for reading text and separating subjects from backgrounds, sends diagnostic information to Google whenever those features run. Google's own disclosure for ML Kit lists the following, which the ML Kit software sends:

  • the device model and operating system version, and the app's identifier and version;
  • a per-installation identifier, which Google describes as not intended to identify you or your device;
  • performance measurements, API settings, event types and error codes, used by Google for diagnostics and usage analytics.

The image and its text are not sent to Google by ML Kit. The information is sent encrypted. Google's handling of it is described in its Android data disclosure and iOS data disclosure for ML Kit. Inwise does not add its own analytics, crash reporting or advertising to this.

5. What Inwise Does Not Do

  • It does not have user accounts, and it does not ask you for an email address, a password or a profile.
  • It does not show advertising, and it does not use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not sell or rent your information.
  • It does not use a camera or microphone without your action, and it does not read your contacts, location or other media.
  • It does not keep a copy of your information anywhere except your phone, your own Google Drive if you connect it, the providers you have chosen, and any message you choose to send us.

6. Permissions

Inwise asks for only what it uses:

  • Camera and photo library (on iPhone, these are asked for when you first need them): to photograph an item, receipt or document, and to pick a picture you already have. On Android, photos are picked with the system photo picker, which does not give Inwise access to your whole library.
  • Notifications, and exact alarms when you choose on-time reminders: to remind you before a warranty ends or a service is due.
  • Internet: for the services you turn on, and for Google Drive backup.
  • Background work on Android: for the weekly Drive backup, and to re-arm reminders after your phone restarts.

7. Children

Inwise is made for adults who keep a record of their own things. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has entered information in Inwise, delete it in the app, or contact us at the address below.

8. How Long Information Is Kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it, or until you remove Inwise from the phone.
  • Google Drive backups: the last three backups are kept in your Drive, until you delete them there.
  • AI and search providers: kept under each provider's own terms and retention period, under the account you set up with it.
  • Google ML Kit diagnostics: kept by Google under its own policies.

Removing Inwise from your phone deletes the app's own data on that phone. It does not delete your Google Drive backups or anything held by a provider, which you delete with that provider.

9. Your Rights

Because Inwise keeps your information on your phone, you control it directly: you can view, change, export and delete it inside the app, disconnect Google Drive, and remove your AI and search keys at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under local law, such as to ask for a copy of information we hold about you, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because Inwise holds no account and no server copy of your information, we can usually act on such a request only through the app, or if you send us the details you want to ask about. To exercise a right, contact us at the address in section 11.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may change this policy when Inwise changes what it keeps or sends. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app or in the store listing before it applies.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or Inwise's data practices, contact support@vbverse.com. Inwise is published by vbverse.
