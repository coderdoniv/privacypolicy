Inwise Support

Inwise is a phone app for the things your household owns: receipts, warranties, reminders and the records that go with them. Everything is kept on your phone. This page answers the most common questions and tells you how to reach us.

Contact

Email support@vbverse.com. Please say which phone you use (Android or iPhone), the Inwise version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please do not send passwords, card numbers or your AI or search keys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a purchase?

Open Inwise, choose Add a purchase, and take a photo of the receipt, pick one from your photos or files, or enter it by hand. Inwise reads the receipt on your phone and shows each item. Check the details, change a category if you want, and choose "They look right" to add them. Items that Inwise was unsure of are marked "Check".

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed, and there is no Inwise server. Adding, finding, reminding and exporting work on your phone without an internet connection. An internet connection is needed only for the services you turn on: your own AI provider, your own web search, and Google Drive backup.

Do I need to pay for anything?

Inwise is free, with no subscription and no item limit. Reading receipts on the phone works without a key. AI answers, pictures found online and "Good to know" search use your own keys with the provider you choose, and that provider may charge you under its own terms.

How do I connect my own AI?

Open Settings, then Your own AI, and choose a provider. Type in your own key from that provider. Keys are kept in your phone's secure storage. Then turn on "Let my AI see receipts" and "Let my AI answer my questions" if you want Inwise to use it. Both are off until you turn them on.

My key is not working. What do I do?

Check that you copied the whole key, that it is still active with the provider, and that the provider's service is available. Then try again. If the problem continues, remove the key and add it again, or contact us.

How do I find a thing I saved?

Use Search and type a word from its name, its category, its place or its serial number. Results appear as you type. You can also open a room from Home to see its things.

How do I set a reminder?

Open a thing, then its warranty, guarantee or service. Set the date. Inwise reminds you before the date passes. For reminders at the exact time, allow exact alarms when Inwise asks, or in your phone's settings for Inwise.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings, then Your data, then Export your data. The screen lists what goes in the file, with counts, and what stays on your phone. Choose Export to save one file that you control.

How do I back up and restore?

On Android, open Settings, then Back up to Google Drive, and connect Google Drive. Inwise asks only for its own hidden folder in your Drive. Backups are made weekly, on Wi-Fi and while charging, and you can back up now. To restore on a new phone, open Inwise on that phone and choose Restore from a backup, from Drive or from a file. A restore can only be made while Inwise holds nothing, and it brings back everything at once.

How do I delete something?

Open the thing and remove it. Inwise asks you to confirm before anything you saved is removed. To remove everything, delete Inwise from your phone; that removes the data kept on that phone. Backups in Google Drive are removed in Google Drive.

Why does Inwise say "Let my AI see receipts" is off?

Inwise does not send anything to an AI provider until you turn that switch on. It is off when you first use the app, so that nothing leaves your phone without your choice.

Does Inwise work on tablets?

Yes. On a wide screen, Inwise uses the space to show more of your things at once.

Something looks wrong on my phone. What should I send you?

Tell us the phone and its operating system version, the Inwise version, and what you tapped before the problem. A screenshot helps. Please do not send receipts or keys unless we ask.
