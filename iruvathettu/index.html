Privacy Policy for Iruvathettu

Effective Date: 27 October 2026

Last Updated: 27 October 2026

Iruvathettu is a private card table for playing 28 with friends and family, on Android and iPhone. You open a table, send one invite, and the people you invite sit down with a name and no account. Empty seats can be filled by bots.

Iruvathettu is published by vbverse. We collect only what the game needs to work, we do not sell personal information, and we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking.

1. Summary

  • No account, email address, phone number, password or social login.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or calendar. The app has no voice feature. Players talk on their own call app, which Iruvathettu never sees or touches.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no purchases.
  • Online table data is kept for a short time and then deleted automatically (see section 6).
  • Nothing is sold, and the only company that handles our game data for us is Cloudflare (section 4).

2. Information We Collect

Information you type

  • Display name (2 to 20 characters). It is shown at the table and in reports. It is often a first name. Names that break our rules are refused (see the Terms).

Information the app creates

  • Player ID: a random identifier created on first launch. It lets the server know which seat is yours, lets you reconnect after a dropped connection, and lets you block or report a player.
  • Player secret: a random 256-bit key that proves a device owns its seat. It is kept in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore on your device. The server keeps only a one-way hash of it, never the secret itself.

Information from a table you play at

  • The table's code and its settings (house rules, seats, bots).
  • Each player's display name and Player ID, the cards played, the moves and the scores, for as long as the table is in use.
  • For a block or a report: the reporter's and the reported player's names and Player IDs, the time, and the reason given.
  • An anonymous match count (the day, the number of people and bots, the house rules used, and whether the game ended early). It has no names, Player IDs or table codes.

Information that reaches our servers in transit

To deliver the game, Cloudflare sees your device's IP address as traffic reaches it. Our game server is designed not to store IP addresses, and it does not log display names, Player IDs or player secrets.

3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following stay on your phone and are never sent to our servers:

  • Your display name, Player ID and player secret.
  • Your settings: language, sound, animation speed and theme.
  • Your block list.
  • Your circles: the groups of other players you have sat with at an online table, shown on the Home screen so you can invite them again. Circles hold other players' names and Player IDs.
  • Any practice game you have in progress against bots.

These are stored in the app's local database on your device. Deleting the app removes them from the device, except that the player secret in the iOS Keychain can remain after the app is deleted (see section 8).

4. Who Handles Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or other third parties.

One company processes game data for us as a service provider: Cloudflare, Inc., which runs Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects and D1 (its database). It processes data on our behalf under its Self-Serve Subscription Agreement and Data Processing Addendum. Cloudflare is the only such provider in Iruvathettu. The app contains no other third-party SDK that collects data.

We may disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect the safety of players or the security of the service.

5. Permissions and Sound

  • Android: the internet permission, to play online tables. The screen is kept awake while a match is on screen, and never in the background.
  • iPhone: no permissions are requested.
  • Iruvathettu never asks for the microphone, camera or location, and its builds declare no microphone use.
  • Its sound effects mix with a call already running in another app, so a card or trick sound never pauses or ends your call. Turning sound off stops all sound from the app.

6. Retention

  • Table data (players, cards, moves and scores) is deleted 24 hours after the table ends. A table that nobody has connected to for 10 minutes ends by itself.
  • Reports (names, Player IDs, time and reason) are deleted after 90 days, by a scheduled job.
  • Anonymous match counts hold no names, Player IDs or table codes and are kept as statistics.
  • On your device, your profile, settings, block list and circles stay until you change them, clear them, or uninstall the app.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can, at any time:

  • Change your display name in Settings.
  • Unblock a player in Settings.
  • Turn sound and animation on or off in Settings.
  • Delete the app, which removes the data stored on your device (see section 8).
  • Ask us, by email, to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to restrict how we use it. Use the contact details in section 10. Because we do not hold an account, we may need your display name and Player ID, and the table code, to find your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR. We will respond to any such request as the law requires.

8. Uninstalling and Reinstalling

Uninstalling Iruvathettu deletes the data stored on your device, with one exception. On iPhone, the player secret in the Keychain can remain after the app is deleted. When you reinstall the app, it replaces any leftover secret with a new one on first launch, so the old player identity cannot be used again. On Android, the app's data is excluded from automatic device backups, so a restored backup does not bring back your player identity.

9. Children and Who the App Is For

Iruvathettu is rated 13 and over. It is made for families and friends, and it is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because players can see each other's display names at a shared table, please make sure any display name you enter is one you are happy for other players to see. If you believe a child under 13 has given us their name or other information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS and WSS).
  • Player secrets are stored on your device in the Keychain or Keystore, and the server stores only a hash.
  • The server limits how often tables can be created, sockets opened and join attempts made, to slow down abuse.
  • Your display name and Player ID are never written to logs.

No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, when our service providers change, or when the law requires it. The Last Updated date at the top of this page will show each change. Where a change alters what we collect, store or share, we will say so in the app's update notes.
