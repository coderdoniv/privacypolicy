Support for Iruvathettu

Last Updated: 27 October 2026

Iruvathettu is a private card table for playing 28 with friends and family, on Android and iPhone. This page answers the questions we are asked most, and tells you how to reach us.

Contact

Email support@vbverse.com. Please say which phone you use, the app version if you know it, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Include the table code if there is one, but never send a password or any other secret; the app does not have passwords.

Getting Started

How do I start a game?

Open Iruvathettu, enter a display name of 2 to 20 characters, and accept the terms. On Home, tap Start a new table. The app gives you a six-character table code and opens your phone's share sheet with an invite message. Send it to your group in any chat app.

How do my friends join?

They can tap the invite link (if they have the app, it opens straight into your table), or they can install Iruvathettu, tap Join with code and type the six characters. The code always works, even when the link does not.

Do my friends need an account?

No. Iruvathettu has no accounts, no email sign-in and no password. Everyone types a display name and sits down.

What if we have only two or three people?

Empty seats can take a bot. Put a bot in any empty seat in the Lobby. Only the host can start the game, and it starts when all four seats are filled by people or bots.

Can I practise on my own?

Yes. Choose Practice on Home to play against bots on your phone.

Playing

What rules does the game use?

The default rules follow Kerala 28 as documented by Pagat. The Rules screen explains them in plain language, with examples. You can read it at any time from the Table.

What are house rules?

House rules are a few settings that families play differently. The host sets them in the Lobby:

  • Pair: the King and Queen of trumps, on or off (on by default).
  • Second-round bidding: bidding after all eight cards are dealt, on or off (on by default).
  • Match target: 6, 10 or 15 game points (10 by default).

Everyone at the table sees the house rules before the game starts. They are locked once the match begins.

My connection dropped. Can I get back into the game?

Yes. The table waits for you for up to 10 minutes. Reopen the app and you return to the same game, in the same seat. Your seat is held while you reconnect.

Can I talk to my friends while we play?

Iruvathettu does not have voice or chat. Start a call in your group on the call app you already use (WhatsApp, or a phone call), then come back to the table. The game's sounds mix with your call and do not pause it.

Can't Join?

The table says it is full, ended or not found.

A table can hold four players. A table ends when nobody has been connected for 10 minutes, and its code can be used again 24 hours after it ends. Check the code with the host, or start a new table.

The app says my name is not allowed.

Names are checked against a list of blocked words in English and Malayalam. Choose a different name. Until the name passes, nothing is created or joined, and you stay where you were. The app never changes or shortens a name for you.

Settings

How do I change my name or language?

Open Settings. Change Display name there, and choose the language under Language: System, English or Malayalam. The language changes at once, and it does not end a game in progress.

How do I turn the sound off?

In Settings, switch Sound off. With sound off, the app plays nothing, including over your call.

Blocking, Reporting and Your Data

How do I block or report a player?

Tap the player on the table to open their Player Sheet. From there you can block them, or report them. Reports are reviewed by us. You can unblock a player in Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling Iruvathettu removes the data stored on your phone. Online table data is deleted by itself 24 hours after the table ends, and reports after 90 days. To ask us to access, correct or delete what we hold about you, email support@vbverse.com. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Does the app need any permissions?

On Android, it needs the internet permission to play online. On iPhone it asks for nothing. It never asks for the microphone, camera or location.

Is Iruvathettu free?

Yes. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no coins.
