Paper Ladder: Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Paper Ladder is a daily word ladder game for Android and iOS, published by vbverse.

What stays on your phone

No account. Your games, streak and stats are stored only on this phone. That covers the words you played, dates and results, hint counts and your settings. The developer never receives this information and has no server that could hold it. The app has no login and no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

Backups

Your phone's own backup may include the app's data. On Android this is Auto Backup to your Google account. On iOS it is your iCloud device backup. These are run by Google and Apple under your own account settings, and the developer cannot see them. You can switch them off in your phone's settings. Backups are what let your streak move with you to a new phone.

Ads

Paper Ladder is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a small banner on screens outside the game, and an optional rewarded video when you ask for an extra hint. There are no ads during play.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, a service run by Google, collects and uses information from your device. Depending on your region and choices this can include your advertising ID or app set ID, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), ad interactions, and diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance. It is used for serving and measuring ads, fraud prevention and analytics. The developer does not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads that use personal data are shown. Where your region requires it, you can change your choice at any time in Settings, under "Privacy and ad choices". On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's Google settings. On iOS the app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and does not read the advertising identifier for tracking.

How Google handles this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Paper Ladder is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are not treated as child-directed.

Sharing your result

If you tap Share, the app hands a short text (puzzle number, steps and par, with no answer words) to the share sheet. Where it goes is up to you and the app you choose. "Report a word" opens your email app with a pre-filled message (app version and the puzzle's words) that you can edit before sending. If you send it, the developer receives that email and your address, and uses it only to reply and to fix word-list problems.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone. Because the developer holds none of it, there is nothing to delete on a server. For ad-related data held by Google, use Google's own controls (see the links above).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published at https://vbverse.com/privacypolicy/paper-ladder/.
