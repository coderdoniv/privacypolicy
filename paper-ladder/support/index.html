Paper Ladder: Support

Need help with Paper Ladder? Email support@vbverse.com. It is a one-person project, so replies may take a few days.

Common questions

Is my streak saved?

Your streak and stats live on your phone only, since there is no account. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) can carry them to a new phone. Uninstalling the app erases them.

A word is missing, or one shouldn't be accepted

Open the game's More menu (three dots) and choose Report a word, or use Settings, About, Report a word. It opens a pre-filled email you can edit and send.

The daily puzzle looks different from my friend's

Everyone gets the same puzzle for the same date, based on the date in your phone's settings. Check that your date and time zone are set automatically.

Ads and privacy choices

Where required, you can change your ad consent in Settings, under "Privacy and ad choices". There are no ads during play. See the Privacy policy.

The rewarded hint video did not load

Check your connection and try again later. The first hint on each puzzle is free and needs no video; if no video is available you get the hint anyway.

Reporting a problem

Please include your phone model, Android or iOS version, the app version (Settings, About) and what happened.
