Privacy Policy for Sylens

Effective Date: 21st May 2026

Last Updated: 21st May 2026

Sylens is a social charades party game for friends, families, and gatherings. Players act out movies while others guess — solo or in multiplayer rooms across phones and Android TV.

Sylens follows a privacy-first approach. We collect only what is necessary to make the game work and never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

Sylens is designed to minimise data collection.

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Anonymous session identifier (used for multiplayer room management)
  • App usage data (features used, screens visited) — aggregated and non-identifiable
  • Crash reports and diagnostics

Data You Provide

  • Team names and player names entered during gameplay (not stored permanently; exists only for the duration of a game session)
  • Room codes used to join multiplayer games

Sylens does not require account registration, email, or any personal identity information.

2. Firebase Services

Sylens uses Firebase services provided by Google to support core functionality.

2.1 Firebase Authentication

  • Sylens uses anonymous authentication only
  • No email, name, or personal credentials are required or collected
  • An anonymous session token is created automatically to identify your device during a multiplayer session
  • This token is not linked to any personal identity

2.2 Cloud Firestore

  • Used to manage real-time multiplayer game rooms
  • Room data (code, team names, game state) is stored temporarily during an active session
  • Room data is automatically cleaned up after the game ends
  • No personal data is stored in Firestore beyond the anonymous session token

2.3 Crash Reporting

  • Used to identify and fix application crashes
  • No sensitive personal data is intentionally collected

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sylens displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob.

  • Ads help keep Sylens free to play
  • AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads
  • Ad personalisation is subject to Google's advertising policies
  • You can opt out of personalised ads via your device settings

Users may purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase to disable all advertisements.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases

  • Sylens offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase
  • All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play
  • Sylens does not collect or store any payment or billing information

5. Camera Permission

Sylens requests camera access only for scanning QR codes to join multiplayer game rooms.

  • The camera is never used for any other purpose
  • No images or video are captured, stored, or transmitted
  • Camera permission is optional — you can also join rooms by typing the room code manually

6. Local Data Storage

  • Sylens stores its movie and content database locally on your device
  • Game preferences (sound, music settings) are stored locally
  • No personal data is stored in the local database

7. Data Sharing

Sylens does not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

Data is only shared:

  • With Firebase (Google) to operate multiplayer features and crash reporting
  • With Google AdMob to display advertisements (unless ads are removed)
  • With Google Play for in-app purchase processing
  • When required by law

8. Data Retention

  • Multiplayer room data is deleted automatically when a game session ends
  • Anonymous session tokens expire and are not linked to any persistent identity
  • Local app data remains until you uninstall the app
  • Analytics data is retained according to Firebase policies

9. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of your data.

You can:

  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Opt out of personalised ads via your Android device settings
  • Revoke camera permission via your device settings at any time

10. Children's Privacy

Sylens is a family-friendly party game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app does not require any account creation or personal information from any user regardless of age.

11. Security

  • Multiplayer data is protected by Firestore security rules — only room participants can access room data
  • Anonymous authentication ensures no personal identity is exposed
  • All communication with Firebase uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)

12. International Data Transfers

Firebase and Google AdMob may process data outside your country. Google applies appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
