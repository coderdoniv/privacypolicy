Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Just Vibing and Squad War can be played without an internet connection because the movie library is stored on your device. TV Party requires an internet connection to synchronize the TV and phones.

How do I start a TV Party game?

On the TV, choose Start a TV Game. The TV will display a QR code and a room code. Players can scan the QR code or enter the room code on their phones to join as controllers.

What happens in Sudden Death?

If teams are tied at the end of the final round in Squad War, Sudden Death breaks the tie with one fast extra round.

What do the in-round assists do?

Change Movie swaps the current movie for a new one. Details reveals the release year, cast, and a short clue. Half Name reveals the first half of the movie title. Each assist costs points from that round's score.

How many movies are in Sylens?

Sylens includes over 1,000 movies across a wide range of genres.

What languages does Sylens support?

Sylens supports English, Malayalam, Hindi, and Tamil.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just Vibing and Squad War require no sign-up. TV Party also requires no account. A temporary game room is created automatically when you start a TV game.

Does Sylens show ads?

Yes. Banner and full-screen ads may appear during play. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that turns ads off permanently.

I bought Remove Ads before. How do I restore my purchase?

Open Remove Ads from the home screen and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is restored using your App Store or Google Play account, so you do not need to purchase it again.

How do I report a bug or suggest a movie or language?

Open Settings in Sylens and select About. You can use the feedback form there to report a bug or suggest a movie or language.

Game Modes

Just Vibing

A relaxed movie charades experience for when you just want to play, act, and have fun.

Squad War

Team-based competition where players battle for the highest score, with Sudden Death available to settle a tie in the final round.

TV Party

Turn your TV into the stage and use your phones as wireless controllers. Players join the temporary game room using a QR code or room code.
